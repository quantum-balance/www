Frequently asked questions

Before your first visit

Is Bio Resonance a medical diagnostic test?

No. Quantum Balance presents Bio Resonance as a complementary wellness service. It should not be used to diagnose disease or replace consultation, testing or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need to prepare for a session?

Ask Isabel directly when booking. Share relevant concerns and let the practitioner know about ongoing medical care or prescribed medication where appropriate.

Can children book sessions?

Children's sessions are offered in Meyersdal. A parent or legal guardian should confirm suitability, consent requirements and the nature of the session before booking.

Should I stop prescribed treatment?

No. Do not stop, delay or change prescribed medication or treatment based on a complementary wellness session. Discuss medical decisions with an appropriately registered healthcare professional.
